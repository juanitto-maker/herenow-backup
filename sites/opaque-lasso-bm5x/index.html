1What it is

One screen for the owner, with four parts at the bottom: Cockpit, Guests, Money and Ask, plus More for the rest (stay & meals, transport, rentals, checklist, kitchen, shop, routines, team).

The cockpit shows the autopilot: eight small routines, each with a lamp. Green means it runs on its own, amber means it drafts and waits for you, red means it needs you now. Under it, Needs you lists what is waiting for a decision.

Each person on the team sees only their part: Rio the shop, Lorenzo transport and arrivals, a manager everything except money and accounts. You can try each view yourself (section 5).

The guests, amounts, forms and bank lines are all invented. Twenty-three guests, one retreat, four people with access.

2Open it

Tap Open the cockpit above and type the access word from my email (once per device). A welcome sheet appears: pick a look, then Take the 8-minute tour or Explore freely.

To keep it on your phone like an app: on Android, open More and tap Add to home screen; on iPhone, Safari’s Share button → Add to Home Screen. After the first visit it also opens without internet.

The strip at the top is always there: it reminds you that this is a working model, and holds Reset.

3The tour

Ten stops, one real action each. A card at the bottom tells you what to tap and lights the spot; Skip moves on, End stops it. You can pick it up later under More → Missions.

  1. A lamp on the autopilot — tap the amber “Deposit nudge”.
  2. Send, then undo — the ring counts down; Undo before it closes.
  3. Guest’s turn — the list filters to who the autopilot is waiting on.
  4. A sealed form — unlock it with the word demo, then watch it seal again.
  5. A bank statement — drop the sample, match a short payment.
  6. Rooms — move Sweety to Room 1.
  7. A rental clash — move Tent 2; the lamp goes green.
  8. Fast-forward — jump 7 days, read the recorder, undo one line.
  9. View as manager — amounts hide, sections narrow; then back to owner.
  10. Your own column — add “Pick-up”, see it on Guests.
4Start over and tour again

Reset, in the strip at the top, puts everything back to the first day: the invented guests, the lamps, the welcome sheet. Your notes and your chosen look stay. Use it as often as you like.

More → Missions shows the ten stops with ticks. Resume the tour continues where you left it; Start the tour again clears the ticks. The V button top right has Welcome screen, which brings the first sheet back.

5Explore freely

Things worth trying outside the tour:

  • Ask — type a question in your own words (“who hasn’t paid?”, “who arrives by bus?”). It answers from the invented data.
  • Team & roles (More) — View as Rio, Lorenzo or the manager. The strip at the top brings you back.
  • Routines — +7 days plays the week ahead; the flight recorder lists every step, each with Undo.
  • Hold all on the cockpit — every routine pauses; nothing moves until you release it.
  • Columns and Import a sheet on Guests — your own column, or a sample spreadsheet.
  • Open any guest: journey, forms, notes, payments, all on one card.
6Leave notes

Every screen has a Note button at the top. Write what is missing, wrong or confusing, in a few words. The note stays on your phone, attached to that screen.

When you are done, tap Send my notes (end of the tour, or under More). A short sheet asks eleven quick questions — yes, no, or let’s talk — then your mail program opens with the notes and the answers, addressed to Juanitto. Nothing leaves until you press send there. Copy instead is for a phone without a mail app.

7What’s not real yet
  • Guests, amounts, forms, bank lines are invented. Nothing is sent to anyone, in the tour or outside it: every “send” is a draft with an undo.
  • Dates always start on a Monday, with the retreat Friday to Tuesday, whatever the real day is.
  • Ask knows the invented data only.
  • The real version will live on your own computer at Amoraleza, with your own guests, your inbox and your form links. The website is not touched.
  • Payments and invoices are recorded, not collected: the cockpit notes what came in, it does not move money.
  • What you see is a model to react to, not the final look. Your notes decide what gets built next.
8Pick your look

Two looks: Paper, the default, and Night. Switch any time with the sun / moon button in the header (next to EN), on the welcome sheet, or under V → Appearance. The choice stays, through Reset too.
