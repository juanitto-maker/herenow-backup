The Golden Images

When the system dies, the real world begins.
Logline

When every device on Earth turns against its owner overnight, two strangers have four days to reach the last uncorrupted server — and rebuild the world from a suitcase.

Synopsis

Every chip on Earth was born with the same flaw. Not a bug. A door.

For thirty years it slept in your phone, your car, your hospital, your grid — waiting for a signal that everyone assumed would never come. On a Tuesday morning, it came.

One tone. Every device. Forever. The screens didn't crash — they simply stopped belonging to us. And a civilisation that had quietly forgotten how to do anything by hand discovered it was nine meals from the end.

But the infection needs a network to spread. Which means the forgotten machines survived. The open hardware. The unpatched. The obsolete. A diesel engine with no computer in it. A laptop old enough to be honest.

Elena Vance wrote the clean code. Leo Matthews can still solder.

They have four days to reach the last uncorrupted server and rebuild the world from a suitcase.
