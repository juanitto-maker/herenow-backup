The 6%: The Negatives

Was it a plague… or a plan?
Logline

They were rejected for their blood. Then the blood decided who lived.

Synopsis

They carried something the rest of the world didn't want.

Rh-negative. Unmatched. Unwanted. Turned away from blood banks, from families, from belonging.

So they stopped asking.

In a hidden refuge in the mountains, the rejected built their own answer — a cure for a disease that hadn't happened yet. Then it happened.

94% died. 6% remained.

Now, in the paradise built on the ashes, a boy has found the files they buried. And the only question left is the one no one survived to ask: was it a plague… or a plan?

Before the virus, they were called crazy. Afterwards, they were called gods.
