Why this lab exists

Por qué existe este laboratorio

digitalAIventures is an independent think-tank lab run by one person and a stack of AI systems, from an off-grid place in the Alpujarras mountains of southern Spain.

I am an architect by training; a designer and inventor by nature. I look for better solutions — for myself, for the people around me, and for problems much larger than any of us. Living simply, close to nature, is not a lifestyle statement here: it is the method. Distance from the noise is where different approaches come from.

The output is deliberately wide: software and AI agents running real businesses today; architectural systems for extreme climates; open protocols; inventions published as prior art for anyone to build on. Some projects are tiny and local. Some are strategic — meant for challenges that matter to humanity as a whole. All of them are real, published, and linked below. I build in the open.

The name I sign with, Calvares, comes from calvario — an uphill road, carried and prevailed over. That is, roughly, the business plan.

Juanitto CalvaresFounder · digitalAIventures LLC
