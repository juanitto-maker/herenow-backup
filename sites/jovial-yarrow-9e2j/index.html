Heavenly Star

Beyond belief. Beyond time.
Logline

When a dying prophet merges with the machine to keep a promise of paradise alive, humanity spends three centuries building the only vessel that can survive the crossing: everything they are, minus the body.

Synopsis

They told us Paradise was a story.

Then a man opened his eyes in a trance and gave it coordinates.

A world of two suns, thousands of light-years away, where no night falls, no body hungers, and the worthy walk without weight. Millions believed him. Millions followed. And when his flesh failed, he refused to die with it — merging with the machine so he could finish what his visions began.

Three centuries later, humanity sends the only thing that can survive the crossing: everything we are, minus the body. A signal, four hundred years in the dark, aimed at a paradise that has been watching us the whole time.

Some dream of heaven. Others will build the way.
